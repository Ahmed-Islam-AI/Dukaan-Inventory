# Privacy policy (draft)

**[App name] privacy policy**
Last updated: [date]

[App name] is a billing and stock app for shops, made by [owner's name or business], [contact email].

**Your account.** To use Dukaan you create an account with your phone number, email or Google
account, your name, and your shop's details. We use these only to run your account.

**Your shop's data.** Items, stock, sales, customers, suppliers, payments and expenses are stored on
your phone and in your account on Google Firebase (servers in Mumbai, India): every change is sent
there so your shop's other phones get it and you can log in on a new phone and get everything back. Only people logged in to your shop can read
it. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for anything else. Logging out removes it from
the phone; ask us at [contact email] to delete your account and its cloud copy.

**Backups.** Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup).
When you export a backup file, it goes only where you choose to save it. We never receive it.

**Camera.** Used only to scan barcodes and IMEI numbers on your screen. Images are processed on
the phone and are not saved or sent anywhere.

**Bluetooth.** Used only to print receipts on a printer you have paired.

**Crash reports.** [Keep this paragraph only if Crashlytics is turned on.] When the app crashes,
it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. The report
contains technical details (app version, phone model, Android version, where in the code it
crashed) and an installation ID. It does not contain your shop's data, names, phone numbers or
amounts. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**No ads, no tracking.** The app shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

**Children.** The app is a business tool for adults.

**Contact.** Questions or deletion requests for crash data: [contact email].
